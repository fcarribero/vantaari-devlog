This is the first playable version of Vantaari: a full solar system to explore and the basics of ship control.

Changes:
- A full solar system to explore: a sun, seven planets, an asteroid belt, a jump gate, and a station.
- Smooth ship movement, with automatic braking on arrival.
- Ships don't collide solidly, but push each other away if they get too close.
- Two camera modes.
- A menu to control your ship: go to a point, approach, hold distance, orbit, or jump into warp.
- Tactical view with a reference grid.
- System map (press M).
- You can save your game.

What's not there yet:
- Docking at stations and object info are just placeholder buttons for now.
- Loading a saved game doesn't restore everything exactly as it was.
- Mining isn't implemented yet.
