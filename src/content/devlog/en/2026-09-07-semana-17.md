---
title: "Week 17: the galaxy starts working without you"
date: 2026-09-07
tags: ["week-17"]
---

The economy stopped depending entirely on the player this week. Mining ships now fly themselves out to nearby asteroid fields to keep factories and refineries stocked with raw minerals, and cargo haulers pick up and deliver open supply contracts on their own, routing themselves across stargates to wherever the goods need to go. It all happens in the background, anywhere in the galaxy, not just around you.

Every planet and moon also got a real look. Instead of a handful of reused textures, each one now has its own surface art matching its type, and you can tell at a glance whether a world could actually support life.

- Fixed an occasional freeze during warp travel near crowded obstacle fields.
- Stars now glow with a color pulled from their own surface instead of a generic yellow, so red dwarfs and other odd stars finally look right.
- Right-clicking a contract's cargo now jumps straight to that contract's window.
- Items in your cargo show stacked badges when they're packed, mission-related, or stolen.
- Redistributed industry structures across the galaxy's systems and shortened stargate names.
- New icons for industry structures and mining laser modules, plus a preview image on structure info panels.
