This week's focus was giving you more to do with your ship.

The big one: there are now sealed crates floating in space with loot inside, and you need to hack them open to get it (breach). For now I built the base system, the idea is to reuse this mechanic for other things later on, but right now it's just about opening these crates.

I also added a big batch of new ship orders: approach a target, hold at a distance, orbit, pick up objects floating in space. Before this there wasn't much control over what you could ask your ship to do, so this opens things up quite a bit.

**Multi-location simulation.** Various fixes to how things get simulated when more than one location is active at once.

**Quick start.** A command to skip straight into the game without going through the intro menu, handy for faster testing.

**Camera and debug tools.** Small tweaks.
