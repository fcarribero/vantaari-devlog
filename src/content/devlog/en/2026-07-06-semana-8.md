This week the focus was grouping characters together and reorganizing the game menu.

Characters can now be grouped. Each group gets its own panel with portraits and shield/hull bars, and you can see who's in your group on the map, with their icon highlighted. It's the base for putting together and coordinating teams later on.

I also reorganized the main game menu: the ship panel and system map now live inside a tabbed menu instead of being separate windows. The game no longer auto-pauses when you open the menu.

**Visual effects.** Tweaks to the tactical view and various effects.

**Combat.** Weapon adjustments.
