This week the focus was drones.

You can now launch drones from your ship: there are five different types, each meant for a different task, and they can be stored, launched, and recalled as part of your ship's loadout.

**Engine trails.** Custom colors based on faction.

**Rendering and more.** Touch-ups to warp, UI, and effects.
