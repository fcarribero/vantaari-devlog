This week I made progress on three different fronts.

Warp travel looks a lot better now: there's a distortion effect when passing close to planets or other large objects, the ship visually slows near massive bodies, and the HUD now shows speed as a fraction of light speed.

There was also a big sound pass: new effects for reloading weapons, launching torpedoes, the ship's engine, the warp countdown, ship destruction, and even a sound for leveling up. There's also music during warp travel.

And the galactic map got a lot of attention: rings around planets, sun glow, shadows cast by planets, and textured orbit lines.

**Map panel.** Improvements to the info it shows.

**Debug mode.** Minor tweaks.
