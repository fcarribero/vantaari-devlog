date: 2026-06-22

This week I made a lot of progress on several fronts.

The main thing was combat. Ships can genuinely shoot at each other now: weapons, damage, shields that absorb hits and regen, and ships can actually be destroyed. I added health bars above ships so you can see how a battle's going at a glance, floating damage numbers when you land a hit, and a combat HUD showing your ship's status. Aggressive enemies now orbit and fire on their own.

The other big thing was the dialogue system. There are now characters with portraits, text that types itself out letter by letter, and voice lines. It starts with some pirates and a tutorial area, but it's the base for adding story and characters going forward.

The rest of the week, a bit of everything:
- You can compare modules before fitting them (a tooltip showing the differences).
- Drag-and-drop modules in your inventory instead of clicking through menus.
- First economy structures: refinery, lab, factory, market.
- Asteroid mining and the first minerals to collect.
- New weapons (missiles, several sizes) and a language selector in the pause menu.
