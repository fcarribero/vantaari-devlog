This week I focused on adding more depth to combat and ship handling.

Weapons now have real ammo: they reload, show how much you have left on the HUD, and there's an animation while reloading. Before this they only had a cooldown, so it adds another layer to managing a long fight.

I also merged the inventory and ship fitting into a single panel, with drag and drop to add or remove modules, swap them, or unfit with a double click. Before this they were two separate windows and it was clunkier to use.

**UI windows.** Position and size tweaks on several windows.

**Selection.** Improved selection ring and a marker for holding distance.

**Notifications.** On-screen toast notifications for different events.

**Language.** Translation and general tweaks.

**Warp and rendering.** Various touch-ups.
