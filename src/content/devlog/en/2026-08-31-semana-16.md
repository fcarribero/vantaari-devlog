title: "Week 16: contracts, sharper targeting, and warp dodging"
date: 2026-08-31
tags: ["week-16"]
---

The big addition this week is Contracts. Pick one up at a station and you pay the shipment's full value upfront as collateral, then the cargo loads onto your ship automatically. Fly it to the destination and deliver it for a reward that covers the collateral plus a bonus. Abandon the contract or let the timer run out and the collateral is gone for good.

Warp travel also got smarter around obstacles. Ships now visibly bank and curve away from planets and other large bodies while cruising past them, instead of looking like they're pointing straight through.

Combat feels more precise too. Shields now light up with a ripple that spreads out from the exact point of impact instead of glowing all over at once, and clicking on a ship checks its real shape, so selection matches what's on screen, including small or distant ships that used to be nearly impossible to click.

**Modules.** Now come in stacks in your cargo instead of one bulky item per slot. Unpack one at a time or all at once to fit them on a ship.

**Production.** Crafting a module now always needs matching-tier materials, closing a shortcut that let some top-tier gear skip top-tier minerals.

**Galactic map.** Site markers switched to real icons, and a new filter panel lets you toggle stations, stargates, mining sites, combat sites, and anomalies on or off.

**Camera.** Opening a station's Market, Production, or Contracts window now smoothly moves the camera into a close-up on the structure.

**Ships.** Updated the Viper and Sparrow I models.
