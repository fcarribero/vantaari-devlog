It's been about a month since I got serious about this project, and it felt like a good time to start this devlog.

I'm a developer and I've been making games for fun since I was a kid. Space4X is something I've wanted to build for a long time: a sandbox space game with a vibe close to EVE Online, where what you do in the world has real consequences: territory control, economy, and who holds power in a given area. Nothing scripted, all of it emerging from what players do.

I work on this in my free time because it's fun, though I've been putting in a good amount of hours. This first month was mostly foundational work: I reorganized how ships and structures are put together internally, so I can add content faster going forward. I also spent a good chunk of time on how warp travel looks and feels, I wanted it to actually feel right.

The rest of the month, in short:
- First icons for ships and stations.
- Camera tweaks and UI window fixes so dragging inventory and menus around doesn't break.
- Started designing asteroid mining and the first economy structures.
- A bunch of small tweaks to overall feel (speeds, effects, HUD).

I'll try to post roughly once a week from here, talking about whatever I worked on. If you have feedback or criticism, it's welcome, honestly I'm looking for it, I want the game to get better.
