This week I added bounty hunting.

There are now pirates with a procedurally generated rap sheet, and each one carries a bounty that scales with how dangerous they are. Defeat one and it drops a claim you can cash in. It's an early version, but it already works end to end.

I also started on factories, economic structures for producing things in the game.

The rest of the week:
- Rendering, enemy AI, and map tweaks.
- Touch-ups to the UI, warp, radar, and ship orders.
